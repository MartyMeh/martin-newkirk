What users value

  • Technical depth. Detailed calculations and technical data help users understand and evaluate solar systems.
  • Ease of use. Written feedback highlights the tool’s clear, intuitive and straightforward interface.
  • Design and experimentation. Users value being able to change assumptions, explore different configurations and experiment with system designs.
  • Professional proposal development. Pro users use Photonik to develop and prototype solar proposals, with positive feedback on its design and calculation capabilities.
