# Ofgem Interview Questions

Questions to ask during the interview/assessment process to better understand the role, team structure, and working environment.

## Preparation: What They Might Ask Me

### About Ofgem & The Digital Delivery Hub

**Ofgem's Mission:**
- Great Britain's independent energy regulator
- Driving toward Net Zero whilst protecting energy consumers, especially vulnerable people
- At the forefront of change across the energy sector

**Digital Delivery Hub:**
- Part of the Delivery and Schemes directorate
- Designs, builds, and maintains digital products and services that support scheme administration
- Works closely with Data, Digital and Security Services (DDSS) to leverage infrastructure and tools like Power BI
- Brought digital team in-house after initially contracting third-party development (learned from BUS experience)
- Focus: Strategic change initiatives, product management, user-centred design, business analysis, architecture, technical support, application support, business and portfolio management

### Major Schemes Administered by Ofgem

**1. Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS) - May 2022 to March 2028**
- Provides upfront capital grants (£7,500 for heat pumps, £5,000 for biomass boilers) for low-carbon heating systems
- **Digital Product:** Installer portal for managing applications, voucher applications, and redemptions
- **Users:** Installers (must be MCS certified)
- **Key Features:** Account creation, voucher management, application tracking dashboard
- **Context:** Ofgem brought this digital delivery in-house after third-party delays - resulted in smoother delivery and positive installer feedback

**2. Energy Company Obligation (ECO4) - April 2022 to March 2026**
- Mandates energy suppliers to deliver energy efficiency measures to domestic premises (low-income/vulnerable households)
- **Digital Product:** Supplier reporting platform for data submission, tracking progress, compliance monitoring
- **Users:** Energy suppliers (obligated parties)
- **Key Features:** Monthly reporting, data collection, compliance tracking, digital documentation with version control
- **Requirements:** Suppliers must submit detailed customer data, energy efficiency measures, photographic/documentary evidence

**3. Warm Home Discount (WHD)**
- Provides financial support to low-income and vulnerable households for energy costs
- **Digital Product:** Eligibility verification, payment processing, compliance monitoring
- **Users:** Energy suppliers, consumers
- **Key Features:** Eligibility verification platforms, monitoring supplier compliance, payment tracking

**4. Energy Redress Scheme**
- Allocates funds from energy company regulatory breaches to support vulnerable consumers
- £40 million made available in December 2024 for charities and community energy groups
- Funds impartial energy advice services and research

**5. Strategic Innovation Fund (SIF)**
- Supports collaborative projects for network development, heat flexibility, resilience, net-zero networks
- Round 4 Discovery Phase opened May 2025 - up to £30 million
- Incorporates digital innovations to optimize energy systems

**6. Data Hub & Data Exchange**
- **Data Hub:** Organisational data store providing common methods to validate, organise, and securely store data (single source of truth for regulatory users)
- **Data Exchange:** External one-stop shop for energy industry, streamlining data submission process and reducing burden on those submitting regulatory data

### What I'd Likely Be Working On

Based on the schemes above, as a Product Manager I would likely be:

1. **Managing digital portals/platforms** for scheme participants (installers, suppliers, consumers)
2. **Overseeing data collection and reporting systems** for compliance and monitoring
3. **Improving user experience** for external users (installers, suppliers) accessing scheme portals
4. **Ensuring regulatory compliance** through digital systems (documentation integrity, version control, audit trails)
5. **Managing stakeholder relationships** with energy suppliers, installers, and internal Ofgem teams
6. **Balancing user needs** with regulatory requirements and compliance needs
7. **Working with legacy systems** while modernizing and improving digital infrastructure
8. **Supporting scheme administration** through efficient digital processes

### Current Challenges Ofgem is Facing

**Digital Delivery Challenges:**
- Competitive digital skills market (led to delays in third-party development, prompting in-house team)
- Balancing innovation with stability and compliance requirements
- Managing legacy systems while modernizing
- Ensuring digital systems meet regulatory compliance needs
- User experience vs. compliance requirements trade-offs

**Scheme Administration Challenges:**
- Ensuring schemes reach vulnerable consumers effectively
- Monitoring and enforcing supplier compliance (e.g., Utilita WHD payment failures in June 2025)
- Managing fraud and ensuring documentation integrity
- Balancing scheme efficiency with thorough compliance checking
- Adapting to changing government policy and scheme requirements

**Broader Energy Sector Challenges:**
- Accelerating progress toward Net Zero targets
- Protecting vulnerable consumers during energy transition
- Managing energy demand, reducing costs, lowering carbon emissions
- Promoting better quality and accessible data across energy network
- Supporting innovation while maintaining regulatory oversight

## Role & Responsibilities

### Schemes & Products
- What schemes would I be working on? Can you tell me about the specific government support schemes the Digital Delivery Hub manages?
- What is the current state of these products - are they legacy systems being maintained, new builds, or a mix?
- How do these products fit into Ofgem's broader mission of supporting Net Zero and protecting energy consumers?

### Tools & Systems
- The tools I'll be working on - are they internal tools for Ofgem staff, external-facing for scheme participants, or both?
- Who is the primary audience for these tools? (e.g., Ofgem staff, energy companies, consumers, installers)
- What is the user base size for these products?
- Are there any specific technical constraints or legacy systems I should be aware of?

## Team Structure & Collaboration

### Team Composition
- How many Product Managers are in the Digital Delivery Hub?
- How many developers/engineers are there? Are they shared across multiple products or dedicated to specific products?
- Are there dedicated designers/UX researchers on the team, or is design handled differently?
- What other roles are in the team? (e.g., Business Analysts, Delivery Managers, Testers)
- How is the team structured - do PMs work with dedicated dev teams or is it more fluid?

### Team Interaction
- How does the team interact day-to-day? What does a typical week look like in terms of meetings and collaboration?
- What does the weekly meeting schedule look like? (stand-ups, sprint planning, retrospectives, stakeholder reviews)
- How closely do you work with the Data, Digital and Security Services (DDSS) team?
- What is the relationship between the Digital Delivery Hub and the broader Delivery and Schemes directorate?

### Cross-functional Collaboration
- Who are the key stakeholders I'll be working with regularly? (internal and external)
- How do you balance competing priorities from different stakeholder groups?
- What does collaboration look like with business users who use these products day-to-day?
